We build and maintain the tools, automation, and infrastructure that keep NYPL's IT operations running. Our focus is on reducing manual work, improving endpoint management, and giving staff reliable self-service tools.

**What we build**

- **Operations portal.** One audited, role-gated console for identity, endpoints, Google Workspace, and infrastructure, built on .NET 10 and Blazor with background jobs on Hangfire and SQL Server.
- **Endpoint management.** Windows imaging, servicing, and app delivery with Configuration Manager and Intune, and macOS management with Jamf.
- **Automation.** PowerShell tools and scheduled jobs for accounts, workstations, apps, and integrations.
